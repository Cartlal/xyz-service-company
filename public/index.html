How a ticket moves

Every ticket is rated by risk before anything changes on your site, and only low-risk fixes run on their own.

Customer reportsFrom your site's "Report a problem" form or our hosted form.
Gemma triagesRated low, moderate or high risk, with a summary and a plan.
Questions if unclearModerate tickets ask the customer for detail, then triage again.
Claude fixesLow-risk fixes are made, checked, built and pushed.
Deployed & solvedCI deploys with a health check; the customer is told it's live.

Connect your website in one request

Post the report from your backend with your client key. You get back a ticket ID and a tracking link for the customer.
